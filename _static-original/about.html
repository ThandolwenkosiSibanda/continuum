Our process

From first call to launch and beyond

01

Discovery & Planning

We begin by understanding your business goals, target audience and project requirements: research, stakeholder interviews and clear objectives, so everyone is aligned.

02

Strategy & Design

Our creative and technical teams develop user-centred designs and a roadmap, including wireframes, prototypes and the technical architecture your app needs.

03

Development & Build

We bring the designs to life in clean, scalable code, working in short iterations with regular testing for quality and performance on every platform.

04

Testing & Quality Assurance

Rigorous testing finds and fixes bugs and polishes usability, so the experience is right before anyone outside the team sees it.

05

Launch & Deployment

We deploy carefully to live environments with minimal disruption, and provide training and documentation for your team as needed.

06

Support & Scaling

After launch we keep supporting and maintaining your app, adapting and scaling it as your business grows.
