AuctionFlow

A complete auction platform (bidder app, website and back office) set up under each auction house's own brand. Live bidding with auto-bids and anti-sniping, hidden reserves, staff roles, and payment by EcoCash, OneMoney or InnBucks. Also built as Beta Auction.

View project

Proqit

A building-materials app built around the customer's building project, not a shopping cart. Guest, trade and bulk pricing, delivery priced by load and route, multi-supplier sourcing, and a WhatsApp assistant that answers in local languages.

View project

Winno Brix

A block yard run from a phone: production batches from moulding to curing, stock by product, quotes and orders, delivery planning, invoices, reports and staff permissions.

View project

Med Compare

Compare medicine prices across pharmacies, cheapest first, and find the one pharmacy that covers a whole prescription for the lowest total. Users save the medicines they buy often and report new prices with a photo of the receipt.

View project

rentr

Rooms, cottages, flats and houses to rent in one app, with the photos, the rent and what's included. Tenants message the landlord and arrange a viewing in the same chat, and landlords list a place in minutes from their phone.

View project

Bushveld Figtree

The point of sale and back office for a hardware store. Staff ring up sales and record change owed, airtime, EcoCash, ZESA and the day's takings; managers track stock with reorder alerts, see reports and set what each role can do. It keeps selling with no signal and syncs when it's back online.

View project

NTAFA

The official app of the Ntabazinduna Football Association. Fans get fixtures, results, the log and squads; officials run fixtures, referee appointments, results, player registration, suspensions and season subscriptions.
