Proqit: building materials, procured like a pro.

A custom app for a Zimbabwean building-materials supplier, built around the customer's building project instead of a shopping cart.

IndustryConstruction
PlatformAndroid & iPhone
ServicesMobile app, admin, integrations
The challenge

Customers don't think in shopping carts.

Proqit sells building materials, but it didn't want to be another online hardware shop. Its goal is to be the easiest way to source materials for a whole building project. Customers think “I'm putting up a house in Bulawayo and I'm at the roofing stage.” A generic e-commerce template can't handle that.

The solution

A system built around how Proqit works.

We built an app where every order belongs to a building project and its stage, prices follow the customer's tier, and delivery is priced by the real load and route. The owner runs orders, drivers, suppliers and quotes from the same app.

What the app does

Features

Projects at the centre

Each build has its stage (foundation, walling, roofing), its documents, its account manager and every order filed against it.

Plans and quotes to beat

Guests send a building plan, bill of quantities or a competitor's quote, and it lands in the team's leads pipeline.

Pricing tiers

Guest, trade and bulk prices, applied automatically.

Delivery by load and route

Bags of cement go by bakkie, roof sheets by flatbed, sand by tipper, each priced accordingly.

Delivery codes

6-digit codes, so goods are only handed to the right person.

WhatsApp assistant

Answers customers in local languages and captures quote requests as leads.

How it's built

Business rules live in the database, not the screen.

React NativeExpoSupabasePostgreSQLWhatsApp Cloud APIOpenAIGoogle Maps
  • React Native and Expo: one codebase for Android and iOS
  • Supabase: PostgreSQL, Auth, Storage, Edge Functions and realtime updates
  • Row-level security on every table, so customers and drivers only ever see their own data
  • Orders re-priced on the server, so totals on the phone can't be tampered with
  • Multi-supplier sourcing that picks the cheapest mix of goods plus delivery
  • A delivery engine that plans routes and picks vehicles by weight, length or volume
  • WhatsApp Cloud API with AI replies, while prices always come from the live catalogue
Why custom

Why custom beat off-the-shelf

Follows the business

The software follows project-centric procurement instead of forcing it into a shop template.

Rules in one place

Pricing, sourcing, delivery and permissions live in the database, so a web dashboard can reuse them later.

Local realities

WhatsApp-first customers, local languages, and delivery zones that grow town by town.

Owned by the client

No per-seat SaaS fees, and the client owns the system.
